A pause, in three steps.

Everything you need to know before you begin.

  1. 01

    Set a little aside.

    Connect your wallet and choose how much DM to put offline. Approve that amount, then confirm your deposit.

  2. 02

    Give it time.

    Your weight is the sum of each deposited token’s time offline. Each ETH funding is shared using everyone’s weight at that moment.

  3. 03

    Return on your terms.

    Collect ETH without disturbing your time. Take back some or all of your DM whenever you want. No deadline. No exit fee.

Rewards come from actual funding, not from the passage of time alone. No funding means no new ETH rewards.

A note on why.

There is a life
between the updates.

The feed has no last page. The market has no closing hour. Somewhere along the way, being connected began to feel like being obliged.

Digital minimalism begins with a small act of authorship: choosing when a tool belongs in your life, and when to put it down.

digital minimalism gives that choice a modest form. A place to set tokens aside, share what is actually funded, and leave without a penalty. A pause that belongs to you.

Our interpretation is inspired by Vitalik Buterin’s “Digital minimalism” ↗, published 31 October 2026. digital minimalism is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vitalik Buterin.

Follow the money.

A public path from trading fees to rewards.

At the normal 3% Stockereum trading fee, 1% goes to the platform and 2% to the creator. The creator manually claims their share and sends it into Offline. Only ETH actually received and allocated by the contract becomes a reward.

The platform’s opening anti-snipe window temporarily charges a higher fee. Creator funding is manual and discretionary; DM does not automatically collect every trade’s fee.

Before you step away.

Does offline mean I have to disconnect?

No. It describes the state of your deposited tokens. DM does not monitor your screen time, internet connection or other wallets.

Can I take my tokens back whenever I want?

Yes. No fixed term, cooldown, exit fee or penalty. Withdraw some or all of your deposit. Normal Ethereum network fees still apply. Already earned ETH remains claimable.

How does time affect my reward?

100 DM resting for two days has the same weight as 200 DM resting for one day. At each funding, your share equals your token-seconds divided by the token-seconds of all current deposits. Time weight has no multiplier tiers or cap.

What happens when I add or withdraw tokens?

Added tokens start at zero time. A partial withdrawal removes proportional time weight. A full withdrawal removes all active time weight; depositing again starts fresh. Collecting ETH never resets your time.

Are rewards guaranteed?

No. Rewards depend on actual funding and everyone’s weight when it arrives. No new rewards accrue between funding transactions. New deposits cannot receive past funding. Withdrawing before a future funding gives no entitlement to it.

Is there anything I need to keep doing?

No check-ins, renewals or compounding. Your deposit and time remain recorded on Ethereum while you are away. Token prices can still change; smart-contract and network risks remain. Read the paper before depositing.
