## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Ordinary Ethereum token holders, including first-time DeFi users. Help them understand Offline, deposit a chosen amount, collect ETH and retrieve tokens. The original creator uses a wallet-gated administrator page to bind a launch and manually fund rewards.

## Confirmed mechanism

Project: digital minimalism (lowercase). Token: DM (uppercase). Flexible deposits with no term, cooldown, exit fee or reward forfeiture. At each ETH funding, allocation uses the quantity and accumulated time of current deposited tokens. Added tokens begin at zero age; partial exit removes proportional age; claiming does not reset age. No new ETH is earned between fundings. Time weight is uncapped. Token launch is last, on Stockereum with an ETH pair and 3% normal fee; 1% platform and 2% creator. Creator manually claims WETH, unwraps it, then funds ETH. Platform Holder Rewards OFF.

## Design direction

Retain the existing black/white, generous negative space, ocean photograph and oversized typographic identity. Use a lowercase digital minimalism wordmark until final artwork arrives. No end-user terminal or CLI. Use familiar buttons and forms with exact labels; no decorative dashboards, neon, gradients, forced tours, daily visits or gamified urgency.

Public surface: follow the user's supplied visual references. Images 1 and 3 define a thin frame, generous negative space, distributed short copy and light lowercase Manrope across a square ocean photograph. Images 2 and 4 define visible circular photographic halftone dots whose radii follow image luminance. Expand the photograph with native scrolling and transition to that dot screen; reverse on upward scrolling. Enter directly into one participation surface without an intermediary action menu. No procedural 3D water, large black panel or nested card. Only Participate / Rewards / About navigation. Keep a visible participation form, optional three-step guide, consistent-block rewards leaderboard, sourced project essay, funding records, contract addresses, FAQ and paper. Admin direct route: /admin, no public menu promotion. Review before signatures. Wallet and chain changes invalidate a review. Loading, empty, disconnected, unavailable, pre-launch and paused states must all be honest.

## Voice

Brand prose may be literary and restrained. Operational and economic prose must be literal and complete. Original project language must not be attributed to Vitalik. Inspiration never implies affiliation or endorsement. No guaranteed returns, fixed APY, verified human disconnection or automatic creator funding claims.
