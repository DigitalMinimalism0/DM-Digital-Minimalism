# DM visual system

Reading this as a redesign for ordinary token holders: quiet, monochrome, spatial branding with familiar product controls. Native HTML/CSS, self-hosted Albert Sans and bundled viem retain the small static-site architecture. Design variance 6/10; motion intensity 6/10 at the scroll entrance and 2/10 in the product; visual density 3/10 on brand surfaces, 5/10 inside the participation form.

## Preserve

Near-black #080808, foreground #f5f5f5, muted text #a1a1a1, panel #101010, rules #303030. The original ocean renderer and its fine, luminance-dependent print grain are restored from the preserved visual version. Its continuous sea shading must remain visible; do not replace it with a screen of bright isolated circles or remove its grain. Distributed navigation, generous negative space and an oversized closing identity retain the existing design’s character. Final logo artwork is pending; digital minimalism is a lowercase typographic placeholder; DM is the uppercase token name.

## Change

Remove terminal syntax, CLI installation and burn ritual. Replace with a visible deposit/withdraw form, separate ETH collection, inline transaction review and wallet selection. Public participation is readable without connecting. Mainnet deployment and pre-launch states never fabricate balances or rewards.

## Typography and shape

Albert Sans 300–600 self-hosted with its OFL license. Body 16px/1.65; functional labels, balance captions, field notes, protocol status and transaction details 14px/1.65. Large identity lettering uses weight 300 and -0.03em tracking; participation headings also use weight 300, with regular body text and medium control labels. Large identity lettering is a brand graphic, not a functional heading. Square controls and surfaces, no decorative shadows or gradient treatments. Text uses sentence case; financial values preserve exact wallet/contract case where useful.

## Motion and accessibility

Native scrolling takes the camera through the original ocean window into the sea, and upward scrolling reverses it. The preserved shader carries the original grain parameters. Water movement stops when the participation form opens. If WebGL is unavailable or lost, the ocean photograph provides the reversible image enlargement fallback. At arrival, the deposit/withdraw form appears directly. Rewards and About are peer views in the same environment. The original form nodes are moved, never recreated, preserving drafts, wallet state and transaction reviews. No intermediate three-action menu. Per the latest correction, the supplied reference-image composition and coarse photographic dot screen are withdrawn. Restore the preceding layout, with a stable opaque #0b0b0b reading surface for all product sections. Sea texture stays outside the text surface. The internal form remains flat, without a nested card. Disabled buttons retain readable captions instead of fading the whole control. Field notes must meet 4.5:1 contrast on the reading surface. Hover and state feedback last 150–250ms; reduced motion skips the camera journey and water motion. No information depends on animation. Keyboard skip link, native controls/dialog/disclosures, visible focus, semantic tab roles with arrow navigation, 44px+ targets, live statuses and readable contrast.

## Responsive

Below 760px, participation stacks; process steps become a vertical sequence; prose and FAQ become single columns. Tables scroll within their own container. The page itself must not overflow at 390px or 320px. Inputs stay at least 16px on mobile. Brand lettering scales independently of product type.
