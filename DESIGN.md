# DM visual system

Reading this as a redesign for ordinary token holders: quiet, monochrome, spatial branding with familiar product controls. Native HTML/CSS, self-hosted Manrope and bundled viem retain the small static-site architecture. Design variance 6/10; motion intensity 3/10; visual density 3/10 on brand surfaces, 5/10 inside the participation form.

## Preserve

Near-black #080808, foreground #f5f5f5, muted text #a1a1a1, panel #101010, rules #303030. A small monochrome ocean photograph interrupts a large lowercase wordmark. Distributed navigation, generous negative space and an oversized closing identity retain the existing design’s character. Final logo artwork is pending; dm. is a replaceable typographic placeholder. No 3D water renderer.

## Change

Remove terminal syntax, CLI installation and burn ritual. Replace with a visible deposit/withdraw form, separate ETH collection, inline transaction review and wallet selection. Public participation is readable without connecting. Mainnet deployment and pre-launch states never fabricate balances or rewards.

## Typography and shape

Manrope 200–800 self-hosted with its OFL license. Body 14px/1.75, supporting copy 11–13px, functional titles 18–40px. Large identity lettering is a brand graphic, not a functional heading. Headline tracking no tighter than -0.04em. Square controls and surfaces, no decorative shadows or gradient treatments. Text uses sentence case; financial values preserve exact wallet/contract case where useful.

## Motion and accessibility

A small pointer displacement moves the ocean window on fine pointers. Hover and state feedback last 150–250ms; reduced motion disables transforms and smooth scrolling. No information depends on animation. Keyboard skip link, native controls/dialog/disclosures, visible focus, semantic tab roles with arrow navigation, 44px+ targets, live statuses and readable contrast.

## Responsive

Below 760px, participation stacks; process steps become a vertical sequence; prose and FAQ become single columns. Tables scroll within their own container. The page itself must not overflow at 390px or 320px. Inputs stay at least 16px on mobile. Brand lettering scales independently of product type.

## Assets

Ocean photograph: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518837695005-2083093ee35b, local web/images/ocean.jpg. Manrope self-hosted, SIL OFL. No remote analytics, scripts or font requests. Repository cover is native vector typography, not a final logo.
